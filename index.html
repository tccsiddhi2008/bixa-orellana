Bixa Orellana

Plant Information

Botanical Name: Bixa orellana (Carl Linnaeus) L.

Common Name: Annatto / Achiote / Sindoor

Family: Bixaceae

Description

A small tree with heart-shaped leaves and red, spiny fruits containing red seeds.

Medical Uses

  • Traditionally used for digestive health and its antioxidant properties.
  • Seeds have been used traditionally for digestive problems.
  • The plant contains antioxidant compounds.
